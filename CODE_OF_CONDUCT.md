# Code of Conduct - Covenant Community Standard

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Jurisdiction:** International

---

## I. Purpose and Scope

This Code of Conduct ("Code") establishes binding standards of professional conduct for all contributors, maintainers, participants, and stakeholders of the Tira-led repository and its associated community ("Project").

The Project is committed to fostering an environment characterized by professionalism, respect, intellectual integrity, and adherence to ethical standards in all collaborative endeavors.

This Code applies to:
- All contributors and maintainers
- All interactions within Project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, etc.)
- All official Project communications and events
- Representation of the Project in public or professional contexts

---

## II. Principles and Standards of Professional Conduct

### 2.1 Professional Integrity

All participants must:
- Conduct themselves with honesty, transparency, and accountability
- Represent themselves and their affiliations accurately
- Avoid misrepresentation of qualifications, credentials, or authority
- Maintain intellectual honesty in code contributions and documentation

### 2.2 Respectful Collaboration

All participants must:
- Communicate in a professional, courteous manner
- Respect the expertise, time, and contributions of others
- Provide and receive constructive feedback without personal attacks
- Acknowledge and credit the work and ideas of colleagues appropriately

### 2.3 Inclusivity and Non-Discrimination

The Project maintains zero tolerance for:
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, national origin, color, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, marital status, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic
- Harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind
- Exclusionary behavior based on personal attributes or beliefs

### 2.4 Technical Excellence

All participants must:
- Adhere to high standards of code quality and documentation
- Provide accurate, well-researched information
- Acknowledge limitations and uncertainties in technical contributions
- Support decisions with evidence and rationale

### 2.5 Confidentiality and Data Protection

All participants must:
- Respect the privacy and security of others
- Refrain from publishing private information without express consent
- Comply with applicable data protection regulations
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive project information when applicable

### 2.6 Legal and Ethical Compliance

All participants must:
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Respect intellectual property rights, including those of the Project
- Adhere to the Project's licensing terms and conditions
- Refrain from illegal activities or contributions

---

## III. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is expressly prohibited and will result in enforcement action:

### 3.1 Harassment and Abuse

- Sustained or deliberate intimidation, threats, or bullying
- Unwelcome advances of a sexual or romantic nature
- Sexual or degrading language, imagery, or commentary
- Deliberate exclusion or isolation of individuals or groups

### 3.2 Discrimination and Bias

- Discriminatory remarks, jokes, or behavior based on protected characteristics
- Promotion of discriminatory ideologies or stereotypes
- Exclusionary policies or practices based on personal attributes
- Systemic barriers to participation or advancement

### 3.3 Violation of Intellectual Property Rights

- Unauthorized use of Project code or materials
- Plagiarism, copyright infringement, or misappropriation of intellectual property
- False attribution or claiming credit for work not performed
- Violation of the Project's licensing terms

### 3.4 Disruptive or Hostile Behavior

- Trolling, spam, or deliberate disruption of Project spaces
- Malicious or unwarranted attacks on individuals or groups
- Persistent and aggressive behavior in violation of community norms
- Deliberate sabotage or interference with Project operations

### 3.5 Misinformation and Misuse

- Deliberate dissemination of false or misleading technical information
- Misuse of Project resources or channels for commercial purposes without authorization
- Social engineering or deceptive practices
- Violation of confidentiality or disclosure of sensitive information

### 3.6 Violation of License Terms

- Commercial use of Project materials in violation of the Project's licensing agreement
- Distribution of Project code outside the terms of its license
- Derivative works used contrary to license provisions
- Circumvention of license restrictions through technical or procedural means

## IV. Reporting and Investigation Procedures

### 4.1 Reporting Violations

Violations of this Code should be reported to the Project maintainers through:

**Primary Contact:**
- Email: lufcresposoliz@gmail.com

**Secondary Channels:**
- GitHub Issues (for public discussions)
- GitHub Discussions (for community concerns)
- Direct message to project maintainers

**Report Requirements:**
Reports should include:
- A clear description of the violation
- Specific examples or evidence (if available)
- Date, time, and location of the incident
- Names and affiliations of involved parties
- Any requested remedial actions

### 4.2 Investigation Process

Upon receipt of a report:

1. **Initial Review (5 business days):** Maintainers will acknowledge receipt and conduct preliminary assessment
2. **Investigation (10-30 business days):** Detailed investigation of the allegation, including interviews and evidence collection
3. **Determination:** Maintainers will make a determination regarding violation and appropriate remedial action
4. **Communication:** All parties will be notified of the outcome and any remedial measures
5. **Appeal:** If requested, maintainers will review the determination within 10 business days

### 4.3 Confidentiality

- Reports will be treated with appropriate confidentiality
- Investigation details will be shared only on a need-to-know basis
- Participants will be protected from retaliation for good-faith reporting

### 4.4 Neutrality and Impartiality

- Investigations will be conducted impartially by designated maintainers
- If a maintainer has a conflict of interest, they will recuse themselves
- All parties will be afforded fair opportunity to present their perspective

## V. Enforcement and Sanctions

### 5.1 Levels of Enforcement

Violations may result in proportionate sanctions:

| Severity | Examples | Sanctions |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Minor** | Discourteous language, minor tone issues | Warning, public or private reminder |
| **Moderate** | Repeated minor violations, disrespectful behavior | Temporary suspension (7-30 days), content removal |
| **Severe** | Harassment, discrimination, IP violations | Indefinite suspension, removal from Project |
| **Critical** | Commercial misuse, legal violations | Permanent ban, legal action |

### 5.2 Specific Sanctions

Depending on violation severity:

- **Warning:** Formal notice of unacceptable behavior
- **Content Removal:** Deletion of comments, pull requests, or issues
- **Temporary Suspension:** Restriction from Project participation for specified period
- **Permanent Ban:** Complete removal from Project and community
- **Legal Action:** Pursuit of civil or criminal remedies for serious violations

### 5.3 Proportionality

Sanctions will be:
- Proportionate to the violation
- Consistent with precedent
- Designed to correct behavior and protect the community
- Subject to review and appeal

## VI. Special Provisions for Commercial Violations

Unauthorized commercial use of Project materials constitutes a material breach of this Code and the Project's licensing agreement.

### 6.1 Commercial Misuse

Commercial violations include (without limitation):
- Selling Project code or derivatives
- Using Project materials in commercial products without authorization
- Offering Project-based services for compensation
- Monetizing Project materials through advertising or subscription models

### 6.2 Remedies for Commercial Misuse

In addition to enforcement actions outlined above, commercial violations may result in:
- Immediate takedown notices and cease-and-desist orders
- Demand for accounting of ill-gotten gains
- Civil litigation for damages, including treble damages
- Recovery of legal and enforcement costs
- Injunctive relief to prevent continued unauthorized use
